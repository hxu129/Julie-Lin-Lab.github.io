Rui Feng

  • Sep 2020 - Jun 2023 M.Sc. in Biological Science, Southern University of Science and Technology, China
  • Sep 2016 - Jun 2020 B.Eng. in Bioengineering, Northwest Agriculture and Forestry University, China
    • I am a PhD student in 2024, focusing on the mechanisms by which RNA editing regulates the circadian rhythm. During my master's studies, I became interested in this field and discovered that A-to-I editing, a type of RNA editing, plays a crucial role in the translation process. Despite this finding, much about RNA editing remains unknown, and I am eager to further explore its regulatory roles in humans. Outside of my research, I enjoy swimming and dancing, which help me unwind and relax both mentally and physically.
